# Colombia: reconciling final asset schedules and helper payments

Use these notes when a Colombian Form 210 preparer later supplies an asset breakdown or the taxpayer asks whether payments to helpers can reduce taxable income. Reconfirm the governing tax year and current law.

## Asset schedules received after the form

1. Sum every schedule component mechanically and reconcile it to the form's rounded patrimonio value.
2. Test each component against primary evidence, not only the accountant's label.
3. For consolidated bank certificates, check whether a standalone account is already included. If the standalone balance plus another account's statement balance equals the consolidated certificate exactly, treat that equality as strong duplicate evidence.
4. For fractional real estate, divide the attributed value by the ownership percentage to recover the implied whole-property basis.
5. Compare the implied basis against acquisition cost, same-year cadastral appraisal, optional fiscal adjustment, and later-year appraisal. A numerical match identifies a plausible method, but remains an inference until the source document is present.
6. For vehicles, distinguish fiscal/acquisition cost from an informational departmental appraisal and from a later-year tax receipt.

## Payments to helpers

Determine income character before evaluating receipts:

- A real, well-supported payment is not deductible if ordinary costs are unavailable against that income class.
- In Form 210, costs associated with capital or non-labor income may be considered when attributable and compliant; work-income costs require special attention to whether the income arose outside employment.
- A salaried employee's private payments to helpers do not become independent-service costs merely because the helpers assisted with employee duties.
- Do not reclassify documented payroll income to non-labor income as a workaround.
- Bank statements prove money moved but generic transfer descriptions do not prove recipient, purpose, hours, service, or tax category.
- Depending on classification, separately verify invoice/equivalent or electronic-payroll support, recipient identity, causal link, withholding, and social-security compliance.
- If direction or subordination may have existed, flag labor-compliance exposure separately. Never fabricate or backdate contracts, invoices, or payroll to manufacture a deduction.

## Final-form verification

When a final PDF resolves a prior dispute, OCR it again and compare the exact fields to the modeled alternatives. A field sequence that matches one alternative exactly is stronger evidence of the adopted treatment than the final tax total alone; isolate remaining one-thousand-peso differences as rounding-order effects.
